The stone

We carve portrait heads, reliefs and architectural fragments from Lychnites marble, the translucent stone the island of Paros has given sculptors for two and a half thousand years.

i.Commission

Portrait in stone

From a live sitting or a 3D scan to a finished head in eleven to sixteen weeks. Roughed by pointing machine, finished by hand with rasp, rifflers and pumice.

ii.Restoration

Conservation work

Consolidation, crack injection and losses replaced in matched stone, documented to museum standard. Recent work for private collections in Geneva and Naxos.

iii.Casting

Plaster & bronze

Silicone moulds taken from finished stone, cast in alabaster plaster or lost-wax bronze in editions of up to eight, each signed and numbered.
