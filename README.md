# Parian Atelier

A stone-carving studio's site. A marble head sits in front of the words and turns, comes close and steps aside as the
page scrolls. At the very end the page goes dark, its eyes open as light and gold runs through the stone.

Serve the folder over HTTP (`npx serve .`). three.js 0.169 loads from jsDelivr.

Head scan: Lee Perry-Smith (Infinite Realities), CC BY 3.0, from the three.js examples. The marble, the veins and the
awakening are shaders in `index.html`.
